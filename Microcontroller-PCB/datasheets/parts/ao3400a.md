## How it's used on this board

- Q1: gate GP14 (PWM7 A) with R2 100k pull-down; drain = LCD LED cathode; LED anode via R1 24 Ω to +5V.

## Gotchas and design notes

- VGS(th) max is 1.45 V, and R_DS(on) is 48 mΩ max at V_GS = 2.5 V (p.2), so a 3.3 V gate turns it fully on (DC-14).
- Gate absolute max is ±12 V (p.1).
- Qg is about 6 nC (p.2). At 1 kHz PWM the switching loss is trivial, and no gate driver is needed.
- Pinout is 1 = G, 2 = S, 3 = D. Check the footprint against this.
