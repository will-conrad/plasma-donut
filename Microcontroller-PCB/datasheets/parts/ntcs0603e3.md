## How it's used on this board

- Off-board, wired to J3–J6. Divider: R4–R7 10k from /VREF_ADC (through solder jumpers JP1–JP4 'PU CUT') on top, NTC to GNDA on the bottom, C18–C21 0.1 µF at the ADC pin.
- Ratiometric: $R = 10\,\text{k}\Omega \cdot N/(4096-N)$, so /VREF_ADC drift cancels.

## Gotchas and design notes

- This datasheet confirms B: the 'L' code in NTCS0603E3103**F**L**T** means B25/85 = **3435 K ±1 %**, and **F** means R25 ±1 % (p.1).
- B25/85 is fitted at 25 °C and 85 °C. Above 85 °C, the Beta model drifts from the real curve, so use Vishay's R-T table (a separate document, not included here) if you need accuracy at MOSFET temperatures.
- Dissipation factor is 3.0 mW/K, so self-heating is negligible in a 10 k divider at 3.3 V (DC-08).
- Thermal time constant is under 4 s (p.1). The reading lags the real temperature by seconds, which matters for over-temperature warnings.
- Rated to 150 °C. For this measurement, the limit is the solder and mounting rather than the part.
