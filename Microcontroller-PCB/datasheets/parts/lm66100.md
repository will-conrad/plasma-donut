## How it's used on this board

- U2: VIN = J1 pin 2 (C1 1 µF), VOUT = +5V (C2 10 µF), **CE tied to VOUT** (always-on reverse-current blocking ✓), ST unconnected.

## Gotchas and design notes

- For always-on reverse-current blocking, tie **CE to VOUT** (p.9). CE tied to GND only gives polarity protection. Never leave CE floating (p.3).
- Tie ST to GND if it isn't used (p.3).
- VIN operating range is 1.5–5.5 V and absolute max is **±6 V** (p.4). A bench supply set much above 5 V will kill it.
- R_ON is 79 mΩ typ / 95 mΩ max at 5 V (p.5), so the drop is a few mV at this board's load.
- Put 1 µF on the input close to the part, and use wide VIN/VOUT/GND traces (p.13–14).
