## How it's used on this board

- U1 channels (from netlist): CH0–3 thermistors (MOS A, MOS B, Coil A, Coil B), **CH4/CH5 /PICKUP_A/B = only a 0.1 µF cap, nothing drives them**, CH6 /PHASE, CH7 /ANALOG_SPARE (J14 via R13 1k).
- VDD = VCC; VREF = /VREF_ADC = VCC through R3 10 Ω with C16 0.1 µF + C17 10 µF. AGND → GNDA, **DGND → GND**.
- SPI0: SCK GP18, MOSI GP19, MISO GP16, CS GP17 (own bus; LCD is on SPI1).

## Gotchas and design notes

- The max clock is only given at 2.7 V (1 MHz) and 5 V (2 MHz), so stay at about 1 MHz at 3.3 V. Don't go below 10 kHz either, or the sample cap droops (p.3, note 3).
- During sampling the input sees 20 pF behind 1 kΩ for 1.5 clocks (p.3, p.17). High-impedance sources need a cap at the pin or a slower clock (see DC-05).
- Connect both AGND and DGND to the **analog** ground plane (p.24). Put a 1 µF bypass right at VDD, and keep clock/digital traces out from under the chip (p.23).
- VREF has to be between 0.25 V and VDD, and it draws 100–150 µA while converting (p.2). That load matters if VREF comes through a resistor (DC-06).
- An input above VDD + 0.6 V exceeds absolute max (p.2). The datasheet gives no injection-current rating (see DC-07).
- Grade: -B is INL ±1 LSB, -C is ±2 LSB (p.2). Check which suffix was ordered.
