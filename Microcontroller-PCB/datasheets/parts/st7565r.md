## How it's used on this board

- The controller inside the NHD LCD. The NHD sheet doesn't cover SPI timing, the command set or absolute max ratings, so go here.

## Gotchas and design notes

- 4-wire SPI is **write-only** (p.24), so the LCD's MISO isn't needed.
- Data is sampled on the SCL rising edge, MSB first, with A0 read on every 8th clock (p.24). SPI mode 0 works, and it's the same mode as the MCP3208.
- SPI clock period ≥ 50 ns at 3.3 V (20 MHz), or ≥ 100 ns at 2.7 V (10 MHz) (p.64). Use ≤ 10 MHz for margin.
- The datasheet warns that SCL is sensitive to reflections and noise (p.24). Near a 13.56 MHz field, that's the reason for the periodic re-init in the firmware brief.
- /RES needs a low pulse of at least 1 µs at 3.3 V and at least 2 µs at 2.7 V (p.65).
